# Fees & Scholarships

- **Merit Scholarship:** Up to 50% tuition waiver for 90%+ in +2 board exams; 25% waiver for 85%–89.9%.
- **First Graduate Scholarship:** 20% tuition concession with valid Revenue Officer First Graduate certificate.
- **Sports Excellence Scholarship:** Tuition waiver for state/national level athletes.
- **Deadline:** Scholarship certificates for 2026–2027 intake must be uploaded by November 15, 2026.
