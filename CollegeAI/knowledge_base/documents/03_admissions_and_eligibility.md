# Admissions & Eligibility Guidelines

## Eligibility Summary
- **B.Sc CS (AI & DS):** 10+2 with Mathematics or Computer Science (minimum 55% aggregate).
- **BCA / B.Sc CS / B.Com CA / BBA:** 10+2 in recognized stream (minimum 50% aggregate).
- **B.Com (Professional Accounting):** 10+2 with Commerce and Accountancy (55% recommended).
- **M.Sc Data Analytics:** Bachelor's degree in CS, Data Science, Math, Statistics, or IT (minimum 55%).
- **Diploma in Applied AI & Cloud Computing:** 10+2 pass or equivalent.

## Application Steps
1. Complete the 3-step Online Application (Personal, Academic, Course).
2. Receive unique reference number (`VETIAS-2026-APP-XXXX`).
3. Track status with reference number and registered email/mobile.
4. Attend counseling with original 10th/12th marksheets, TC, Aadhaar, and quota certificates.
